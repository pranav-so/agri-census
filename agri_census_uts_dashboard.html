Three stories jump out of this data:   Lakshadweep and D&N Haveli are almost entirely tribal farming economies (95% and 89% ST respectively) — a stark contrast to Delhi and Chandigarh where ST holdings round to zero. Chandigarh is an outlier on tenure: 81% of its holdings are jointly operated, the highest of any UT. And Daman & Diu and Lakshadweep are extreme smallholder economies — over 92% of all farms are marginal (<1 ha).
